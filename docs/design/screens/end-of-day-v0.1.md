## Current report and hierarchy

Current Owner-only `GET /api/reports/end-of-day?date=YYYY-MM-DD` returns aggregates for a selected **Store-local business date**: `businessDate`, canonical IANA `timeZoneId`, `[startUtc, endUtc)`, `salesRevenue`, `collected` (Sale Payments, Customer Debt Payments, actual Customer Refunds, net amount), `customerOutstandingDebtAtEnd`, `supplierPayments` (Purchase Payments, Supplier Debt Payments, total), `supplierOutstandingDebtAtEnd`, and `estimatedGrossProfit` (Net Sales Revenue, Historical COGS, amount, `costReliability`). The current [EndOfDayView.vue](../../../src/frontend/simplestore-web/src/views/EndOfDayView.vue) predates D-101; its existence does not complete this redesign. D-052 keeps the full financial report and Estimated Gross Profit Owner-only; Store isolation and backend authorization remain authoritative.

Show the business date and `Store.TimeZoneId` near the report title. Date picker and previous/next date are presentation improvements over the existing date query. A date list is visual direction and needs data/API review if more than client-side date selection. Browser/server-local time and the UTC calendar date do not define the business day (D-054). Display the local window and its **đến trước** exclusive end in detailed explanation.

Lead with **Doanh thu bán hàng** (`salesRevenue`), **Tiền thu thuần** (`collected.netAmount`) and **Lãi gộp ước tính** with visible CostReliability, then show **Công nợ khách hàng cuối ngày**, **Công nợ nhà cung cấp cuối ngày** and **Thanh toán nhà cung cấp** where useful. Revenue is economic Sale/correction value; Collected is actual money movement, including old-debt collection and subtracting actual refunds (D-049/D-053). Do not use identical labels or visual treatment that makes them interchangeable. Ending debt is the balance at the historical cutoff, never debt newly created on that date, collected cash or Revenue. The mock **Công nợ phát sinh** is not an EOD API field: historical new-debt-created is `FUTURE / REQUIRES API + SEMANTIC REVIEW`, and D-069 Today semantics must not be generalized silently.

Historical COGS comes from authoritative SaleLine cost snapshots/correction basis, not today's Product average cost. **Lãi gộp ước tính = Net Sales Revenue − Historical COGS** (D-050); call it neither net nor accounting profit. It excludes salary, rent, electricity, depreciation, tax and other operating expenses. Show `Reliable`, `Estimated` or `Unavailable` plainly beside the metric, with a Vietnamese explanation when cost evidence is uncertain. **Historical End-of-day SaleCount** is `FUTURE / API REVIEW REQUIRED`; D-070 only establishes Today counting semantics.

## Data and interaction boundaries

The payment-method composition chart is `APPROVED VISUAL DIRECTION / API SUPPORT PENDING`: current response has collection components, not the Cash/Transfer breakdown. A future definition must include the relevant Sale payments, Customer DebtPayments and refund direction, and reconcile to Net Collected. Revenue by Category is `FUTURE / DEPENDS ON CATEGORY DOMAIN + REPORTING REVIEW` under D-097; infer no Category from Product names. Detailed transaction tables, typed source evidence and charts beyond current aggregates are `DESIGN DIRECTION APPROVED / API + TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION PENDING WHERE NOT CURRENTLY EXPOSED`. Never calculate headlines from a partially loaded detail page.

D-048 remains authoritative: End-of-day is a **query/report**, not accounting close, state transition, day/period lock, cash closing balance or Close Day/Reopen Day flow. Do not render mock **Đã đóng/Chưa đóng**, close actor/time or “số liệu đã khóa” as real states. See the [separate future concept](end-of-day-future-close-v0.1.md). Historical report values may reflect later corrections under approved event-date and cutoff rules; no immutable snapshot is promised.

Design distinct loading, zero-activity, API failure, invalid date, authorization failure, unavailable reliability, unavailable drill-down and incomplete chart-data states. On desktop retain clear KPI/detail hierarchy; stack cards on tablet and present readable sequential sections on mobile rather than squeezing tables. Later implementation may refine spacing, typography, chart sizing, table density, panel dimensions, accessibility and wording, while preserving D-048, Revenue/Collected separation, debt cutoff, historical COGS, CostReliability and Owner-only access. Material behavior changes require Product Owner review.
